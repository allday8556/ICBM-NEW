M5 (`test 12`, `test 13`) were canary products and were deleted afterwards (M5 §10.2), so no
ICBM-registered listing is on sale. The owner decided that the acceptance uses the KM holding

proven by a read-only seller-code search, bound to the existing canonical Item of its source product,
never a REGISTER registration (M6E-05). The record below uses that population, and `KM190` as its
worked example.
