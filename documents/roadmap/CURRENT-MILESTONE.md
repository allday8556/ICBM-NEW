**Standing authorization (ADR-0020).** That per-slice authorization is met by the ROADMAP standing authorization of `documents/decisions/adr/0020-roadmap-standing-authorization.md` when a slice satisfies all of its conditions: it is the next step in ROADMAP order read fresh from the exact main, the canonical documents already decide its scope, safety invariants and owner boundary, it is provider-zero, any endpoint adoption or schema change is already decided by a canonical contract, and it is its own PR that passes CI, the GPT exact-head audit and the independent Claude cross-audit. A real provider call, LIVE, a real canary, the residual-risk acceptance and every approval of §7.2 still need the user's explicit decision. What ADR-0020 also left to a user decision — an architecture or implementation-policy choice, conflicting canon on an implementation matter, a scope detail, a data model — is decided as the next paragraph says.

**Operating authority (ADR-0022).** `documents/decisions/adr/0022-agent-operating-authority.md` and `documents/rules/14-operating-authority.md` widen that standing authorization: work the canonical documents already define is carried out without a per-step user decision, whether or not it meets every condition of ADR-0020. Its implementation choices — internal architecture, a data model, a schema or migration for an already-approved feature, an endpoint shape, a scope detail, which reading of conflicting canon an implementation follows — are the agent's, and are verified by the GPT exact-head audit and the independent Claude cross-audit. The user's explicit decision is still needed for, and only for: a real provider call, LIVE, a real canary, a real supplier or provider read whose acceptance needs its own grant, the residual-risk acceptance, a cost, a transfer of real data, every approval of §7.2, a product feature the canonical requirements do not contain, an undecided product direction, a change beyond the user's requirements, and a hold the user places on a PR themselves, which stops that PR until the user lifts it.
